Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026 · Last updated August 7, 2026

This policy explains what Bella collects, why, and who we share it with. It covers our website at bellacoach.com and the Bella coaching application at app.bellacoach.com.

Who we are

Bella is operated by Nick Fairbanks, doing business as Bella Coach ("Bella," "we," "us"). We provide AI-powered coaching software for dental treatment coordinators.

The best way to reach us about anything in this policy — including a request to access or delete your information — is by email at nick@bellacoach.com. We will provide a postal address on request.

The short version

  • We collect what you send us through our contact form, and standard analytics about how people use our website.
  • We use a Meta (Facebook) advertising pixel on our website. We do not use it in the application.
  • Bella is a tool for dental staff, not for patients. It is not intended for patients to use directly.
  • Where a practice enables patient-data features, that data is governed by a signed Business Associate Agreement, and it is never sent to our AI providers.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Information we collect

When you contact us through this website

Our "Book a walkthrough" form collects your name, email address, practice name, role, and anything you write in the notes field. Submissions are delivered to us through Formspree. We use this only to respond to you and to follow up about Bella.

Automatically, when you browse this website

  • Advertising and analytics. We use the Meta Pixel, which records page views and reports them to Meta along with your IP address, browser and device information, and referring page. Meta may combine this with information it already holds about you to measure and target advertising.
  • Server logs. Our host, Vercel, records standard request logs including IP address, user agent, and requested URL.
  • Fonts. Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google and discloses your IP address on each page load.

When you use the Bella application

  • Account information — name, email address, role, and the practice or office you belong to.
  • Coaching activity — practice roleplay conversations, scores, case reviews, and progress history generated as staff use the product.
  • Treatment and financial records — only in workspaces where a practice has enabled those features. See Patient information below.

Patient information

Bella is built for dental staff to rehearse and review conversations. It is not a patient-facing product and patients do not have accounts.

Some features let a practice record real treatment estimates, which can contain patient information. Those features are off by default and are enabled only for practices that have signed a Business Associate Agreement with us. Where they are enabled:

  • Patient information is stored in our database under access controls that restrict it to authorized staff at that practice.
  • Patient information is never sent to our AI providers. Where the product uses AI over practice performance, it receives de-identified figures only — dollar amounts, percentages, and counts — never names, dates of birth, or contact details.
  • Our case-review feature is designed without any field for a patient name, date of birth, or contact information.

If you are a patient and believe a practice has entered your information into Bella, contact that practice directly. They control that record, and we act on their instructions.

Cookies and tracking

The Meta Pixel sets cookies on this website in order to recognize returning visitors and attribute advertising. You can limit this in several ways:

  • Adjust your Meta ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • Block third-party cookies in your browser settings.
  • Enable your browser's tracking-protection or "Do Not Track" feature. Note that we do not currently respond to Do Not Track signals.

The Bella application does not use advertising or analytics trackers. It uses only the cookies required to keep you signed in.

How long we keep information

  • Contact-form submissions — 24 months from the date you contact us.
  • Account and coaching records — for the life of the subscription, plus 90 days after it ends.
  • Treatment and financial records — governed by the Business Associate Agreement with that practice and by the practice's own retention obligations, which may be longer than the periods above.
  • Server logs — retained on our infrastructure providers' standard schedules.

Practices may request deletion of their workspace data at any time, and you may ask us to delete your personal information at any time using the contact details above.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our infrastructure providers. Access to production data is restricted, and access to patient information within the application is enforced per practice and per role at the database level. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Some privacy laws grant these rights only to residents of particular states or countries. We do not draw that line. We honor the following requests from anyone who asks, wherever you live:

  • Access — ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix information that is wrong.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of advertising — see Cookies and tracking above.

Email nick@bellacoach.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will not charge you or treat you differently for making a request. You may unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the link in the message.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the advertising cookies described above.

If your information was entered into Bella by a dental practice, that practice controls that record. We will refer your request to them and help them act on it.

Children

Bella is a workplace tool for dental professionals. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website or the application.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated to account holders directly.
