Treatment presentation, from the photo to the number

A treatment presentation is a short sequence: show the problem, explain what happens if it waits, say what the treatment does and what insurance pays, then say the number. Most of what goes wrong happens when the order breaks — the price arrives first, the printout does the talking, or the insurance explanation turns into jargon. Start with value before price; the other posts go deeper on each step.
