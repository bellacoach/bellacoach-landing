Objection handling, one sentence at a time

Most dental cases are not lost to a hard no. They are lost to a polite sentence that sounds like a delay — “I need to think about it,” “let me check with my husband,” “can we just do the one tooth?” — and to what the coordinator says, or fails to say, in the seconds after it. These posts take one objection each and show what it usually means, how to find out, and the words that keep the case alive without pressuring anyone.
