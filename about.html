About Bella

Bella is AI coaching software for dental treatment coordinators. It gives them reps on the conversation that decides the case — the treatment-plan presentation — before they have it with a real family, and then coaches them on the consults they actually had.

What we make

  • Bella Coach — role-play against AI patients who push back, scored against your own playbook.
  • Bella Treatment Desk — chairside estimates, follow-ups and case-acceptance tracking by coordinator and office.
  • Bella Voice — with everyone’s consent, coaching on recorded consults, behind a signed Business Associate Agreement.

Why it exists

Treatment coordinators are the biggest lever on a practice’s production, and most of them learn the job by shadowing, on real families, with the revenue on the line. Bella moves the reps off your patients. The features came from real consults that went badly and from wanting to know why, which is also what the blog is about.
