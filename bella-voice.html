Questions

What practices ask about Bella Voice

Do patients have to agree to being recorded?

Yes, and it is captured before anything records. California is a two-party consent state, so this was never going to be a checkbox in a settings screen — it is part of the workflow.

How long do you keep the audio?

Ninety days, then it is deleted. The transcript and the coaching notes survive it deliberately: the value is in what was learned, not in keeping a copy of the conversation.

Can we use the rest of Bella without it?

Yes, and most practices will start that way. Role-play needs no patient data at all, so Bella Coach runs on day one while the agreements for Voice are still being worked through.
