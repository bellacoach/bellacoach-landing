Questions

What practices ask about Bella Treatment Desk

Does it replace our practice management software?

No. Bella does not run your schedule, your clinical notes or your claims. It is the case conversation and what happens to the case afterwards.

Can pricing differ between our offices?

Yes. Treatments and pricing are set per office, which is the usual reality for a group where fee schedules were never identical to begin with.

What happens to patient information?

It sits behind the same agreement gate as the rest of the patient-data side: no BAA in place, no estimate saved. That is enforced in the software, not left to a policy someone has to remember.
